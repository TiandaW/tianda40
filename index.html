Dress code

the wrong occasion

It's a 40th in a paddock, so come dressed for anything but. The more committed, the better. A few ideas:

  • Black tie
  • Ski trip
  • Snorkelling
  • Job interview
  • Year 12 formal
  • Grand final
  • Somebody's wedding
  • Office Christmas party
  • Beekeeping
  • Bedtime

Whatever you pick, bring a jumper to match. May nights get cold out here.

Camping

Pitch up from 9am Saturday, or Friday night if you're coming a long way. Tents, swags, vans and caravans all welcome.

What's here

  • Two toilets and showers
  • A camp kitchen
  • Milk, tea and basic coffee
  • One big communal fire pit

What to bring

  • Drinking water. There's no power or water at the campsites.
  • Your tent, swag or van
  • Warm layers and a good sleeping bag
  • Earplugs if you sleep early. There's no quiet time.

No campfires at your own site, please. Bring a chair to the big one instead.

Bringing a dog?

Dogs are welcome if they get along with other dogs, and either leave birds alone or stay on a lead. There's no fencing and the neighbours have livestock, so come with a plan to keep your dog from wandering.
